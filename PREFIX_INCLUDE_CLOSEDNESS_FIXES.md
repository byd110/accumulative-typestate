# Prefix-Include Closedness Proof Notes

Date: 2026-06-16

This note records the adjustment made while preparing the prefix-include
shuffle-closedness proof for Rocq.

## Paper Argument Reviewed

The paper proof argues that if every base word in `L` is a prefix of some chosen
word in `L`, then any two base seeds are comparable as prefixes. It then assumes
one seed is a prefix of the other and reasons about an interleaving by partitioning
the shuffled word at the points where new seed letters become necessary.

This is a reasonable mathematical outline, but two parts are too implicit for a
direct Rocq proof:

1. The "without loss of generality" step needs an explicit comparability lemma.
2. The partitioning step needs a constructive witness, not just an informal
   appeal to the first new occurrence or the longest suitable prefix.

## Mechanized Adjustment

The current Rocq development proves the reusable framework:

```text
singleton_reseeding_complete L ->
shuffle_closed (pumping_closure L)
```

and a prefix-specific reduction:

```text
prefix_include L ->
comparable_reseeding_complete L ->
shuffle_closed (pumping_closure L)
```

The comparability part is now mechanized:

```text
prefix_include L ->
L seed1 ->
L seed2 ->
prefix_of seed1 seed2 \/ prefix_of seed2 seed1
```

This formally justifies the paper proof's comparison of two base seeds.

## Remaining Constructive Lemma

The missing prefix-specific mathematical content is now isolated as
`comparable_reseeding_complete L`. In words, it says:

```text
If two seeds from L are comparable by prefix,
and out1 is pumpable from seed1,
and out2 is pumpable from seed2,
then every interleaving of out1 and out2 is pumpable from some seed in L.
```

For a prefix-include language, the expected seed is the longer of the two
comparable seeds.

To complete the full theorem, the paper's partitioning argument should be
replaced by a constructive Rocq lemma that scans an interleaving and builds the
required seed-cover witness directly.

## Follow-Up Attempt

I tried to close `comparable_reseeding_complete` directly from the existing
`pump_semantic` characterization. The natural mathematical lemma is:

```text
if p is a prefix of q,
out1 is in p's singleton pumping closure,
out2 is in q's singleton pumping closure,
and out is an interleaving of out1 and out2,
then out is in q's singleton pumping closure.
```

This should be true. However, a direct induction on `pump_semantic` gets stuck at
exactly the point glossed over in the paper proof: after consuming the first seed
letter, the shuffle may alternate between allowed pumped blocks and future seed
letters from either side. The proof must construct the split between "still in the
current pumped block" and "now entering the next seed milestone."

There are two viable Rocq-friendly routes:

1. Add a tagged interleaving/erasure lemma.
   Prove that a pumping step inside one side of an interleaving can be replayed
   as a pumping step, or finite pumping closure, of the interleaved output. Then
   the main theorem follows by induction over the two pumping-closure
   derivations, using only a base-seed shuffle lemma.

2. Add a greedy seed-cover decomposition.
   Define a constructive function/relation that scans an output word and splits
   it into seed milestones plus pumped blocks. Then prove that shuffling a word
   covered by a prefix seed with a word covered by the longer seed is covered by
   the longer seed.

The second route is closer to the prose proof. The first route may be more
reusable for the later permutation-extended family.

## Remaining Work

The current Rocq file proves the generic reduction:

```text
prefix_include L ->
comparable_reseeding_complete L ->
shuffle_closed (pumping_closure L)
```

The remaining task is to prove `comparable_reseeding_complete L` for
prefix-include languages without assuming it.

Concrete obligations still needed:

1. Prove a base-seed shuffle lemma:

```text
prefix_of p q ->
interleaving p q out ->
out is in the singleton pumping closure of q
```

This says that shuffling a shorter prefix seed with a longer prefix seed can be
generated by pumping from the longer seed.

2. Lift the base-seed lemma from base words to pumped descendants. One possible
shape is:

```text
pumping_closure {p} out1 ->
pumping_closure {q} out2 ->
prefix_of p q ->
interleaving out1 out2 out ->
pumping_closure {q} out
```

3. Choose a constructive strategy for obligation 2:

- Tagged interleaving route:
  introduce a tagged/intermediate representation of a shuffle, prove erasure
  correctness, and show that pumping steps in either component can be replayed
  inside the erased shuffle.

- Greedy seed-cover route:
  define a relation or function that scans the shuffled output and extracts the
  milestone letters of the longer seed, with all skipped letters justified as
  already available pumped letters.

4. Once obligation 2 is proven, instantiate
`comparable_reseeding_complete L` by selecting the longer comparable seed:

```text
if prefix_of seed1 seed2, choose seed2
if prefix_of seed2 seed1, choose seed1
```

5. Combine that instance with the existing theorem:

```text
prefix_include_pumping_closure_shuffle_closed_from_comparable_reseeding
```

to obtain the final ad-hoc closedness theorem for prefix-include pumping
closures.
